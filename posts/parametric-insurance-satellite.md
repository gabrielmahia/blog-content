---
title: "Parametric Insurance: When Satellite Data Replaces the Claims Adjuster"
tags: africa, ai, mcp
---

Traditional crop insurance requires a claims adjuster to visit a farm. In Kenya — 47 counties, farms averaging 1.5 hectares — this is economically impossible at scale.

Parametric insurance triggers on satellite data (NDMA rainfall deficit, NDVI scores) instead. If the trigger fires: automatic payout to M-PESA within 72 hours. No claims process.

bima-mcp implements this with ACRE Africa methodology.

```bash
pip install bima-mcp
```
