Every conversation about AI in Africa converges on the same framing: "AI for Africa." Apps that use AI. AI everything.

That framing is backwards.

The structural problem East Africa faces is a shortage of coordination infrastructure: insurance, credit scoring, market price signals, reputation systems, civic accountability. These are the systems that underpin economic participation everywhere they exist — and their absence locks people out of formal economies.

AI is how you deploy them at scale, in Swahili. But AI is the delivery layer. Not the product.
