---
title: "Building an Alternative Credit Score from M-PESA Behavioral Data"
tags: africa, ai, mcp
---

FICO took 50 years of credit card data. 70% of Kenyan adults have no formal credit history. But they have M-PESA.

mkopo-mcp builds a 300-850 credit score from M-PESA behavioral signals:
- Income regularity (30%)
- Payment consistency (25%)
- Savings behavior (25%)
- Tenure + history (20%)

```bash
pip install mkopo-mcp
```
