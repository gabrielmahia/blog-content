40%+ of Kenyans don't know their constitutional rights. The information exists — it's just locked in dense English legal language.

hakiyangu presents both the government position AND citizen rights on any constitutional question, in Swahili or English, using a 3-agent adversarial format (arXiv:2305.19118).

Try it: hakiyangu.streamlit.app
