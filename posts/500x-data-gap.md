arXiv:2509.04516: AI models produce 4x more errors in Swahili than English. Root cause: 500x less Swahili data in Common Crawl.

The window for establishing Swahili data primacy is 12-18 months. AI is entering self-improving phase (Anthropic RSI, May 2026). Whatever language advantages exist today compound forward.

Four Swahili datasets on HuggingFace (CC BY 4.0): civic NLP, agricultural Q&A, legal corpus, health protocols.
