title: "Why East Africa Needs Coordination Infrastructure, Not Just AI Apps"
tags: africa, ai, mcp

Insurance. Credit scoring. Market price signals. Reputation systems. Civic accountability.

These aren't AI features — they're the coordination technologies that underpin economic participation. Their absence locks people out of formal economies.

AI is the delivery layer. The product is the infrastructure.

**The Five Coordination Failures:**
- Uncertainty → Insurance (bima-mcp)
- Trust gap → Credit (mkopo-mcp)
- Info asymmetry → Markets (soko-mcp)
- Trust compression → Reputation (sifa-mcp)
- Enforcement → Legal access (civic-agent-kit)
