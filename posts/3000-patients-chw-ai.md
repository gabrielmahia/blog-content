Kenya has ~100,000 Community Health Workers for 54M people. Rural ratio: 3,000:1.

afyanipoa is an AI clinical co-pilot built on:
- Kenya MOH CHW protocols (fever, cough, diarrhoea, ANC, malnutrition)
- WHO IMCI integrated management guidelines
- arXiv:2408.17216 (federated AI on Raspberry Pi-class devices)

DEMO — labeled "Si uamuzi wa daktari / Not a doctor's decision" on every screen.
