---
title: "Reputation Portability: The Missing Infrastructure for Economic Mobility"
tags: africa, ai, mcp
---

A skilled plumber in Chicago can move to LA and work immediately. Her Yelp reviews and contractor license travel with her.

A skilled mason in Mombasa cannot take her reputation to Nairobi. Trust is tribal and local.

sifa-mcp (sifa = reputation in Swahili) builds portable worker profiles with verified service records and trust scores.

```bash
pip install sifa-mcp
```
